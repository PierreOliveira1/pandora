# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short, we expect everyone who takes part to:

- Be respectful of other people, their experience and their point of view.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, insults and personal attacks.
- Respect privacy: do not publish someone's private information without permission.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space the project uses.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads or block accounts that break this code.

The full text, including enforcement guidelines, is at [contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
